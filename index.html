Map Information

The information shown on this map is for guidance only and must not be used for safety-critical decisions.

MyIM links only refer to the main item of equipment. They may not show faults allocated to associated equipment, such as stretcher bars or backdrives. Current open faults are also not shown. These limitations are due to restrictions with how faults are stored within MyIM.

Attempts have been made to show all equipment, but it is possible some may have been missed.

For corrections, suggestions, or errors, email alex.corcoran@networkrail.co.uk.
